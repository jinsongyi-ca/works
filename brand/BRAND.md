# Sonny Jin

**SONNY JIN** · Songyi · 金松毅
Marketing, brand & business strategist. *When the answer isn't there yet, I go find it.*

This is the personal brand book behind Sonny's website, résumé, LinkedIn and decks. One rule sits above the others: **the results are the hero, the brand is the frame.** The site is a career told as proof: every screen shows a problem that had no answer yet, and what Sonny did about it.

## Positioning

> When the answer isn't there yet,
> **I go find it.**

Always set on two lines, broken exactly there. The first line is quiet (`ink-muted`), the second is the landing: bold, in solid `champagne`. Use it once per surface, never paraphrased.

It is backed by four abilities, which the site proves story by story:

**PROBLEM SOLVING | ANALYSIS | STRATEGY | EXECUTION**

Set in capitals, separated by thin vertical rules, never boxed or numbered.

## Name

- Wordmark and site header: **SONNY JIN**, in capitals with wide tracking.
- Opening screen: **SONNY JIN** large, in solid `champagne`. It does not react to the cursor. The given name sits diagonally off the top right corner of SONNY as **(Song-yi)**: about one eighth of the name's size, in parentheses, hyphenated, title case, in `champagne-deep`.
- **金松毅 is an inscription, not a caption.** Set vertically (top to bottom) in LXGW WenKai to the right of the English name, its first and last characters aligned with the top and the baseline of SONNY JIN. No seal under it. It never sits under the name as a horizontal line.
- Never "Songyi Jin" alone in a headline. Never reverse the order.

## Chinese, sparingly

The brand speaks English. Chinese appears only in the 金松毅 inscription, the 金 in the seal, and where the work itself is Chinese (a bilingual ad, an album title), shown as it ran.

## Voice

Write hooks, not reports. The site's job is to make someone open a story, then ask about it in the interview.

**The mix: 80% storyteller, 20% editor.** Most lines are first person and show a scene: the problem as it felt at the time, then what was done about it. About one line in five is a cool, magazine-style statement that lets a fact land on its own. Never lead with a bare string of numbers.

| Storyteller (most lines) | Editor (the occasional line) |
| --- | --- |
| Nobody knew if New York would show up. So we asked a billboard. | No market data. So we asked Times Square. |
| Carmakers didn't need another website. They needed shelf space. I built it. | Top five on Google. Then the homepage became a product. |

Rules:
- **I or we.** "We" for team work (Live Nation, NEO SOUL); "I" for the part that was Sonny's own: the client he signed, the call he made, the product he packaged.
- **Three beats per highlight: THE GAP, THE MOVE, THE RESULT.** One or two lines each. The interview is for the rest.
- **Numbers support, they don't lead.** One headline number per highlight, set large in `numeral`.
- **Claim only what is true, and what is a strength.** No "sold out" as a headline: a sold-out show also means demand that went unmet.
- **Plain grammar.** Short sentences. No dashes as punctuation, no stacked adjectives, no buzzword chains.

## Colour

Three layers, each with a clear job.

**1. The frame (Sonny's own colours).** Opening screen, transitions, full timeline, close.
- `paper` `#FAFAF8`, a clean near-white. Never the old cream.
- `ink` `#0B0C0E` for text and big numerals; `ink-muted` `#5E6268` for the quiet line, labels and captions.
- `champagne` `#C3A986` is the seal fill and Sonny's signature colour. As text on paper it is allowed only at display size and only twice: the name SONNY JIN and *I go find it*. Never for body text, labels or captions (contrast is too low). `champagne-deep` `#A88B5F` edges the seal, sets (Song-yi) and draws the cursor.
- `line` `#E4E3DE` for hairlines.
- `gold-metal`: a static metallic gold gradient (`#8C6F42` → `#D9C39A` → `#A88B5F` → `#CDB386` → `#8C6F42`). Used only for the hidden 金 where the cursor reveals it. No sparkles, no moving glint.

**2. Highlight takeover (the employer's colours).** When a highlight is on screen, the whole screen takes the brand colour of the company Sonny worked for: Maple Diversity in Maple's colours, Live Nation in Live Nation's, and so on. Text on a takeover is white or near-black, whichever passes 4.5:1. The employer palette list is kept in `Takeovers` below.

**3. Evidence (the client's colours).** Inside "See the evidence", each piece of work takes the colour of the client it was made for: the IKEA item in IKEA blue and yellow, the Tissot item in Tissot's. A client's colours never take over an employer's highlight.

**Naming a subsidiary.** The rail uses the name people recognise (Aramark). The role line and the timeline use the legal employer, so it matches the résumé and any background check: "Complete Purchasing Services, an Aramark company" on the highlight, "Complete Purchasing Services (Aramark)" on the timeline. Never a dash between the two names.

Order: NEO SOUL, Maple Diversity, Live Nation, SparkLease, Aramark, CAOTAI Music. Live Nation and Aramark are both red fields, so they are kept apart and their accents differ (black chips vs white chips).

**The accent rule (one treatment for every highlight).** The accent is always a fill, never a text colour:
- Beat labels (THE GAP, THE MOVE, THE RESULT) sit on a small accent chip, with dark or white text, whichever reads.
- The headline number has an accent marker band across its lower third; the part of the number over the band switches to the chip text colour.
- Lit ability tags take an accent underline.
Body text never takes the accent. Backgrounds crossfade over 600ms.

The NEO SOUL gold is brighter and yellower than Sonny's `champagne`, so the employer never reads as Sonny's own frame.

Dark theme (`Night`) keeps the same roles on carbon `#0B0C0E`: warm-white text, champagne allowed as text there (8.7:1).

## Type

| Family | Face | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `serif` | Caladea | All English. The free, metric-compatible twin of Cambria. |
| `cn` | LXGW WenKai 霞鹜文楷 | The vertical 金松毅 inscription and any Chinese in the work. |

**Capitals, and only where they help.** Use capitals with wide tracking (`label`) for: the name (SONNY JIN), navigation, the four abilities, beat labels (THE GAP, THE MOVE, THE RESULT), buttons and small labels.
Keep normal case for: the positioning line, hooks and body text, **and brand names**, which keep their own spelling (IKEA, Tissot, Live Nation, Mercedes‑Benz) because their case is part of how people recognise them. This includes employer names on the highlight rail: NEO SOUL, Maple Diversity, Live Nation, SparkLease (one word, S and L capital), Aramark, CAOTAI Music.

Typeface may still change; the rules above are what matter.

## Interaction

The cursor is the brand's light. Three behaviours, all quiet, all off when the visitor asks for reduced motion. Only the background 金 changes colour; the name and the positioning line never react.

1. **Finding the 金.** The 金 sits large in the background at almost no contrast, pushed to the right so it bleeds off the edge. Around the cursor it turns to `gold-metal`, a still metallic gradient. The visitor literally finds the hidden answer. This is the signature moment.
2. **Brand names come alive.** On hover, each name in the "worked with · pitched to" line grows slightly and turns its own brand colour.
3. **The cursor.** A small `champagne-deep` dot replaces the arrow and opens into a ring over anything clickable.

## Layout

- **Desktop first.** Wide margins (64px), generous air, one idea per screen.
- **Square corners** everywhere except the seal (3 units on its 200‑unit square).
- **Hairlines, not boxes.** The four abilities are divided by thin vertical rules; sections by `line` and space.
- **The work is one click away, and it shows.** Each highlight ends with a work card: three tilted thumbnails of the real work, an accent chip "SEE THE WORK →", and a count line ("14 pieces · Times Square, Posters, Venues"). On hover the thumbnails fan out and the arrow moves. It opens a panel that slides in from the right, so the story stays in view.

## Logo

The mark is a seal carrying one character, **金**, from the calligraphy of **Yan Zhenqing (颜真卿, 709–785, Tang dynasty)**. The 捺 of the roof was redrawn to swell evenly; the edges keep the texture of the stone rubbing. The source is in the public domain.

- **Seal** (`logos/sj-seal.svg`): champagne square with a fine `champagne-deep` edge, 金 in near-black `#14161A` (8.7:1), filling about 90% of the face. Favicon, avatar, inscription, sign‑off. Minimum 16px.
- **Wordmark** (`logos/sj-wordmark.svg`): seal + SONNY JIN in tracked capitals, outlined. Site header, résumé, decks.
- **Glyph** (`logos/sj-jin-glyph.svg`): the 金 alone in champagne, for the hidden background and large graphic use.
- Do not put the seal on a champagne fill, recolour it, rotate it, or set white 金 on champagne (unreadable).

## Iconography

No icon set. Words in `label` capitals instead ("SEE THE WORK →", "READ THE NYT ↗"). The only glyphs are the arrows ↗ and →.
