NEO SOUL Entertainment

Co-founder

The music was in China. Its audience was already in America.

The gap

Chinese students across the US had almost nothing to go out for, while Chinese indie music was having its moment back home. Nobody was bringing the two together.

The move

We brought the artists to American stages and built each tour city by city. There was no market data, so I built forecasting and pricing models from streaming, social and box office benchmarks to pick venues, size cities and price tickets.

The result

19 concerts, forecasts within 5%. US$600K in revenue, US$250K raised, and a US$6.3M valuation in two years.

Maple Diversity Communications

Strategic Partnerships Manager

Nearly one in four Canadians was born abroad. Most brands still spoke to one Canada.

The gap

Brands knew multicultural Canada mattered, but few had the data or the cultural know-how to reach it. Maple had to prove the case to win new clients.

The move

I turned NielsenIQ, Vividata and Statistics Canada data into the case for each brand: who the audience was, where to reach them and what to say. I pitched 11 brands across CPG, automotive, tech and the public sector, and automated prospecting so my time went into proposals.

The result

I signed IKEA and Tissot myself, and won new projects from Aveeno, Listerine, Audi and Panasonic. Revenue grew 14% year over year, and a rebuilt vendor pipeline cut localization costs by 25%.

SparkLease

Product Marketing Manager

Carmakers didn’t need another website. They needed shelf space.

The gap

SparkLease reached 100,000+ active users a year, with nothing packaged for the carmakers who wanted to reach them.

The move

I grew organic leads with SEO and AI search optimization, then turned the site’s screen space into a tiered product for OEMs and their dealers.

The result

Leads up 19% a month on average, top five on Google across 5 keyword groups. Seven cold pitches to BMW’s agency, Mercedes‑Benz and GM brands; three made the shortlist.

+19%Organic leads, month over month

Aramark

Program Marketing Manager · Complete Purchasing Services, an Aramark company

Same events, every month. Planned from memory, every time.

The gap

We ran online and in-person events for 2 B2B programs serving 500+ client accounts. The events repeated, but there was no SOP. Every step lived in someone’s memory.

The move

The events were cookie cutter, so I made them repeatable. I built an automated project management system in Smartsheet: enter the event date, and every action item appears with its own due date.

The result

Coordination time fell by more than half, and every new event started from the same plan. For our 50th anniversary, I helped put clients and the brands we represented in one room, with a trade show to make business happen.

50%+Less coordination time

Artist Operations Manager
