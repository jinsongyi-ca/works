NEO SOUL

Co-founder · 2016–2020

NEO SOUL Entertainment · A pioneer in bringing East Asian live music to North America. Los Angeles.

The music was in China. Its audience was already in America.

The gap

Chinese students in the US had little to go out for. Chinese indie music was booming back home. Nobody connected the two.

The move

We brought the artists to US stages. With no market data, I built forecasting and pricing models to pick venues, size cities and price tickets.

The result

19 concerts, forecasts within 5%. US$600K revenue, US$250K raised across two rounds, and a US$6.3M valuation in two years.

Maple Diversity

Strategic Partnerships Manager · 2023–2024

Maple Diversity Communications · Canada’s leading multicultural marketing agency.

Nearly one in four Canadians was born abroad. Most brands still spoke to one Canada.

The gap

Brands knew multicultural Canada mattered, but few had the data or cultural know-how to reach it. Maple had to prove the case.

The move

I turned NielsenIQ, Vividata and Statistics Canada data into a case for each brand, pitched 11 brands, and automated prospecting to focus on proposals.

The result

Signed IKEA and Tissot myself; won new projects from Aveeno, Listerine, Audi and Panasonic. Revenue up 14% year over year, localization costs down 25%.

Live Nation

Team Lead, Strategy Consultant · 2022–2023 · MBA consulting engagement

Live Nation Entertainment · The world’s largest live entertainment company. Fortune 500.

Seven of us. Seven reports. One roadmap the client kept.

The gap

Live Nation wanted growth in markets it hadn’t entered. Nobody had mapped what stood in the way.

The move

I brought Live Nation on as a client and led seven people through the diagnosis: industry, business model, organization, execution.

The result

A seven-year, multi-scenario roadmap projecting 36.8% CAGR in untapped markets. The client adopted it.

SparkLease

Product Marketing Manager · 2026 · Contract

SparkLease · Canada’s go-to Chinese-language car leasing platform, with 100,000+ annual active users.

Carmakers didn’t need another website. They needed shelf space.

The gap

100,000+ active users a year, and nothing packaged for the carmakers who wanted to reach them.

The move

I grew organic leads with SEO and AI search, then turned the site’s screen space into a tiered product for OEMs and dealers.

The result

Leads up 19% a month, top five on Google. Seven cold pitches to BMW’s agency, Mercedes‑Benz and GM brands; three shortlisted.

Aramark

Program Marketing Manager · 2025

Complete Purchasing Services, an Aramark company · Group purchasing for senior living, healthcare and education across Canada, for 50 years.

Same events, every month. Planned from memory, every time.

The gap

Our events repeated every month for 500+ client accounts, but there was no SOP. Every step lived in someone’s memory.

The move

I built an automated system in Smartsheet: enter the event date, and every action item appears with its own due date.

The result

Project management time cut by more than half. I also helped run our 50th anniversary: clients, partner brands and a trade show in one room.

50%+Less project management time
